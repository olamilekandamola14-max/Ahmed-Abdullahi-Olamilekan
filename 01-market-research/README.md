# 01 — Market & Competitive Research

Business immersion and competitive analysis comparing SIDONPAY's planned positioning (a Nigeria-first, multi-currency digital wallet) against **Wise**, **Chipper Cash**, and **Revolut** — the three competitors closest to SIDONPAY's own multi-currency consumer-wallet model, as opposed to merchant-payment platforms like Paystack or Flutterwave which solve a different problem.

## Key finding

None of the three competitors combine transparent pricing, fast KYC, and local (Nigeria-first) bill payment in one product. Chipper Cash turned out to be the closest real competitor — it already offers peer-to-peer transfers, a USD virtual account, and free local bill payment — which reframed SIDONPAY's differentiator from "first to offer local bill pay" to "first to pair the full bundle (bill pay + USD account + card + gift cards) with Wise-style published pricing."

## Method

Competitor data gathered via research on published fee structures, KYC requirements, and product features, synthesized into a 4-criteria positioning matrix (pricing transparency, product features, customer acquisition, onboarding ease).
